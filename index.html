How to Use

Get AI-powered trade analysis in 3 simple steps.

Step 1: Upload Your Chart

Take a screenshot of your trading chart from TradingView, TopStep, NinjaTrader, or any other platform. Drag and drop it into the upload zone or click to browse files.

Step 2: Choose Analysis Type

Enter Now: Get immediate entry with stop loss and take profit levels for quick scalps.

Wait for Optimal: Get a better entry price to wait for with improved risk/reward.

Step 3: Get Your Analysis

Within seconds, receive AI-generated analysis including trade direction (LONG/SHORT), confidence level, entry price, stop loss, take profit, risk/reward ratio, and detailed reasoning.

📸 Tips for Best Results

  • ✅ Use clear, high-resolution screenshots
  • ✅ Include visible price levels on the Y-axis
  • ✅ Show recent candles (last 50-100 candles work best)
  • ✅ Include any indicators you use (RSI, MACD, etc.)
  • ❌ Avoid blurry or cropped images
  • ❌ Don't upload images without price data
